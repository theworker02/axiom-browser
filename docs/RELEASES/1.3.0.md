# Axiom 1.3.0 — Settings foundation

## Highlights

- Expanded profile-persisted browser settings across Appearance, On startup, Search, Privacy &
  security, Cookies & site data, Downloads, Languages & accessibility, Permissions, and System &
  developer controls.
- New trusted `axiom://settings` dashboard makes each category visible without pretending that
  unimplemented browser APIs are active.
- Official orbital-A brand continues across the native window, executable icon, new tab, and
  public documentation.

## Chrome comparison

Chrome groups settings around personalization/accessibility, privacy/permissions/security, and
performance/utility. Axiom adopts comparable categories while retaining local-first defaults:
there is no AI integration, telemetry, tracking beacon, account requirement, or private-data
upload. Axiom does not claim Chrome's full permissions, Safe Browsing, translation, PDF, GPU, or
accessibility implementation; preferences for those future surfaces are persisted and labelled as
such rather than faked.
